About CHUBB
CHUBB (Channel Usage Based Backoff) is a load-aware alternative to BEB proposed for dense IEEE 802.11 WANETs.

Each node tracks its own transmission ratio:
N_TRatio = N_t / T

This ratio selects an adaptive multiplicative factor G from Table 3 of the base paper, replacing BEB's fixed doubling step. In dense networks (N > 10) this reduces unnecessary backoff growth and improves channel efficiency.

Reported gain: 3–31 % throughput improvement over BEB in simulated WANETs.

Ref: HCIS Journal, Vol. 14, 2024 — KUST Kohat
Model Assumptions
Discrete-time DCF model
  • Slot time σ = 20 µs (802.11b)
  • SIFS = 10 µs, DIFS = 50 µs
  • Ideal channel — no hidden terminal
  • Saturated & non-saturated traffic mix
  • Packet arrival: Bernoulli(λ) per slot
  • Retry limit: 4 stages (matching 802.11 RL=4)
  • ACK assumed instantaneous on success
